Moses Mayfield - After a month at Longview Farm Studios in rural Massachussets, I'm at The Mix Room in Burbank, CA with producer Ben Grosse finishing up the Epic debut for Moses Mayfield. Think Coldplay meets Pearl Jam meets Dave Matthews Band.

 

Yellowcard - I'll be back with producer Neal Avron at Sunset Sound to do the new album for Yellowcard.

 

Curious - I'm producing a 3 song demo for Rinat, a new artist who's got new wave hooks to spare! Expect Moog keyboards, chugging 8th note guitars, melodic bass playing and more...

 

Aeon Spoke - I'm at Glenwood Place Studios in LA with Aeon Spoke tracking and mixing a few more songs for their upcoming release.

 

The Exies - The Exies have just finished their second full length for Virgin Records with your truly helping out. Expect a release on November 30th, '04 with the first single "Ugly" going to radio this Oct. 5th.

 

Days Away - Just finished at Ocean Studios in Burbank, CA working on the debut album for Days Away on Lava Records. Check out their website at www.daysawaymusic.com.

 

Kazi - I'm re-mixing a great new band out of Austin called Kazi who combine U2 melodies with Interpol drumbeats and Cure-like guitars. Add some Ratatat drums from me and we're ready to rock...

 

Biirdie - I just finished 5 more songs with Birdies to complete their new full-length. They combine the boy/girl singing aesthetic with Brill Building/Beach Boys style songwriting to a very unique effect. Read and hear all about it @ www.flyawaybirdie.com.
