This page should probably be called "recent and current projects," cause a lot of them are finished, but I'm sure you get the idea....

 

Pop Levi - I finished engineering and mixing the new Pop Levi album entitled "Never Never Love." Recorded at Westbeach Recorders and Westlake Studios in Hollywood, California. A sunny record made by a band from rainy ole Liverpool. And mostly done in the room that also made "Off the Wall" and "Thriller," two large influences on the sound.

 

Whole Wheat Bread - I produced, engineered and mixed the new album entitled "The Hearts of Hoodlums" both at the label's remote studio outside Orlando, Florida and at Skyline Studio, my personal spot here in L.A. It's an innovative record that melds punk with dirty south crunk and features guest appearances from Lil Jon and Tim Armstrong.

 

Heartstop - I produced, engineered and mixed a 6 song EP for this Southern Californian modern rock band, featuring classic songwriting bathed in sheets of distorted guitars and crashing drums. Two songs are getting play on L.A.'s influential radio station KROQ.

 

Leena - She's a great songstress from Australia who flew all the way over here to do her record at Sunset Sound Recorders in Hollywood, California with producers Joe Chicarrelli and Mike Daly along with me as engineer. I got to break out all my Daniel Lanois and Tchad Blake tricks for this one!

 

The Invincibles - Punk/Crunk band Whole Wheat Bread gets together with L.A. rapper Murs to do a collaboration album called "The Invincibles." I'll be producing, engineering and mixing, and we'll be going for an innovative sound that's part rap, part punk and part ska. They played the Coachella festival recently and rocked the tent! Check out their myspace page for videos of it....

 

Armor For Sleep - I'll be back over at The Mix Room with Ben Grosse to complete AFS's first major label album. They're a great heavy emo band that has emerged from the Warped Tour scene selling shitloads of their first independent album and now takin' it to the next level.

 

Grace Potter and the Nocturnals - Grace has the pipes of Janis Joplin and the smokey hue of Cat Power. Combine that with a sound from her band that reminds me of Neil Young's Crazy Horse and you've got Grace Potter and the Nocturnals. I'll be working on their new album with Mike Daly and Joe Chiccarelli over at Glenwood Place Studios in Burbank.

 

Curious (pt. 2) - Ben Grosse and I will be producing 2 more songs with new-wavers Curious. This time we'll be leaning to a more modern and "British" approach. Drum tracking to be done at Ocean Way with overdubs at Ben's place, The Mix Room.
