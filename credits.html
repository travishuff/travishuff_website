My writing credits include television mainly, but also a few independent films. I've written for "E: True Hollywood Story" with episodes including "The Real World," "Courtney Love," "Ellen DeGeneres," and "Heather Locklear" and recently did more music for "E: THS Investigates." I wrote the theme song for a pilot on the new High Definition TV channel called "Moov" which was recently reported on in the New York Times talking about television's next wave. The show is called "fLOW" and should be a unique experience. My old band, The Rosenbergs, also had a few of our songs placed in "Dawson's Creek" and "Party of Five."
