Georgia - I engineered and mixed a 3 song demo with this band and producer Mike Daly which just netted them a deal with Atlantic Records. Congrats guys! Imagine the harmony singing of the Bee Gees with the sonic stylings of The Eagles and The Black Crowes.

 

Pop Levi - Four lads from Liverpool, England. Signed to the experimental label Ninja Tune, they combine the songwriting ideas of Prince and The Beatles to their own unique sound. I engineered and mixed a bunch of B-sides for their upcoming album and am set to do the next full length this summer...

 

Lil Jon - Tracking punk-crunk band Whole Wheat Bread as Lil Jon's backing band for his new album "Crunk-Rock," which already has the single "Snap Yo Fingers" doing crazy play at radio. We're working at Henson Studios while Jon's also got a MIDI studio at his rented house in the Hollywood Hills. Yeaaaahhhhh!

 

Lavender Diamond - I'll be engineering this record for producer Thom Monahan (Devendra Banhart, Vetiver) at Stagg St. Studios in Los Angeles, CA. Part of the new "freak-folk" scene led by Devendra Banhardt.

 

Reeve Oliver - I'm heading back over to Sunset Sound with producer Neal Avron for tracking dates of this new rock band from San Diego. For fans of Yellowcard and Foo Fighters, something else to check out...

 

Whole Wheat Bread - I'll be heading to Cell Studios in Orlando, FL to produce and engineer an EP with Whole Wheat Bread that combines their punk rock energy with their hip-hop/crunck heart. If you've seen them live, then you know what I'm talking about. If not, then stay tuned...

 

The Rosewood Thieves - Spending a few days at Stagg St. Studios in Los Angeles with this new band on Artemis Records for their debut album. We're working with producer Thom Monahan of Pernice Bros. and Devendra Banhart acclaim. Think White Album meets The Band meets ? and the Mysterians.

 

Fall Out Boy - I'll be heading into Ocean Studios, Burbank, CA with producer Neal Avron to begin recording Fall Out Boy's major label debut on Nov. 22nd. Rolling Stone named them one of the top 10 bands to watch in 2005!
